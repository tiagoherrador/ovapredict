# Current status and roadmap

## Current status

OvaPredict is an **experimental proof of concept (TRL 3)**. The authorized conference material reports that quantitative morphospatial profiles were associated with the benign–borderline–malignant diagnostic spectrum in the studied cohort. These are preliminary findings and should not be interpreted as a validated diagnostic, prognostic, or treatment-selection tool.

## What the current work supports

- Quantitative description of tissue-image heterogeneity at an aggregate research level.
- Generation of testable hypotheses about image-derived phenotypes and biomarker signals.
- A basis for reproducibility and validation studies in computational pathology.

## What must happen before clinical use can be considered

1. **Independent validation:** evaluate reproducibility in external and, ideally, multicenter cohorts.
2. **Pathology review:** assess the biological and histopathological interpretation of the identified patterns.
3. **Clinical evaluation:** define and test clinically meaningful endpoints with an appropriate study design.
4. **Robustness studies:** characterize performance across staining, imaging, site, and cohort variation.
5. **Governance and translation:** establish data governance, prospective validation, and the relevant regulatory pathway before any clinical deployment.

These are prospective validation goals, not claims of completed work.
